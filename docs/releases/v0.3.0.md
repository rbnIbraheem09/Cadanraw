Cadanraw 0.3.0 is a redesign of everything around the board: faster to get around, nicer to look at, and yours to make your own. The board itself, and how it pans and zooms, are unchanged.

### Find anything, do anything

- **⌘K** searches boards, folders and every command. Type a name that doesn't exist and it offers to create that board. **⌘P** jumps straight to a board.
- **Press / on a board** to insert a block right under the pointer. Type to filter, or paste a link to drop in a link card.
- Insert and Share now live in the board's toolbar, so the board gets the full width of the window.
- More shortcuts: **⌘1** Home, **⌘,** Settings, **⌘.** Focus mode. The full list is in Settings.

### Quick note, from anywhere

- **⌃⌥Space** (you can change it) opens a small note window over whatever app you're in. Write, press Return, and it's filed onto an **Inbox** board, or onto the board you have open. A lone link becomes a link card.

### Make it yours

- **Seven themes:** Slate (the original), Midnight, Ember, Forest, Plum, and two light themes, Paper and Glacier. Pick an accent colour too.
- **Five board backgrounds:** Dots, Grid, Crosses, Isometric and Blank, each with a strength setting. They all track panning and zooming exactly like the dots always have.
- **A proper Settings page:** theme, background, sounds, motion, what to show on launch, the quick-note shortcut, assistant (MCP) access, and your data folder.

### Start faster

- **Templates:** Brainstorm, Mind map, Project plan, Moodboard, Storyboard, Weekly planner and Meeting notes, from Home or the New board dialog.
- **Boards reopen where you left them.** New and imported boards open fitted to their content.

### Feels better

- A new title bar with breadcrumbs, inline rename and a quiet saved indicator; a simpler sidebar; a calmer Home with live board previews.
- Spring motion throughout, the board card growing into the board when you open it, **Focus mode** to hide everything but the board, soft interface sounds, and a small celebration when you tick the last box on a checklist. Sounds, motion and celebrations can each be turned off.

### Fixes

- Restoring a version from the History right-click menu now actually restores it. Before, the open board could save its old content straight back over the restore.
- Drawing no longer makes the sidebar re-render on every pointer move, which helps on big boards.
- Renaming a board from Home no longer shows a folder form.

### Before updating

- Your boards, folders, versions and library are untouched. Settings are new and start at their defaults: the Slate theme and Dots, as before.
- Agent access (MCP) has moved from the right-hand panel to **Settings → Assistants**. Its setup and saved configs keep working.
- **⌘K** now opens search. To add a link to a shape, use the right-click menu or "Add a link to the selection" in search.
- If the quick-note shortcut is already used by another app, Settings says so; pick a different one there.
- Desktop installers are unsigned. macOS may require right-click Open; Windows may show SmartScreen on first launch.
